Grammar counting rules:
- Count concrete occurrences, not every broad prerequisite category. For example,
  "Could I get a coffee?" may demonstrate Questions, Modal Verbs and Articles; do not
  automatically add Parts of Speech, Sentence Structure, Nouns and Word Order merely
  because every sentence contains them.
- Use the canonical topic name paired with the selected ID.
- Evidence must quote the smallest relevant span from the user's utterance.
- In incorrect mode, issue must concisely describe the error. In correct mode it is null.

Vocabulary rules:
- Consider only common nouns and adjectives actually used by the learner.
- Exclude pronouns, verbs, adverbs, determiners, proper names, and words appearing only
  in dialogue history or feedback.
- Assign CEFR level A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, or C2 to the word sense used in context.
- Lemmatize the word and preserve its observed form in surface_form.
- In incorrect mode, include only a noun/adjective with a genuine lexical, form, sense,
  or collocational error. Grammar-only article/agreement errors do not make the noun or
  adjective a vocabulary error.

Idiom rules:
- Include only established idioms or strongly conventional figurative expressions.
- Do not label ordinary collocations, phrasal verbs, polite request frames, or literal
  phrases as idioms.
- normalized_idiom is the lowercase dictionary form used as its stable database key.
- In incorrect mode, include only an attempted idiom that was used incorrectly.

Return only the structured JSON requested by the response schema. Empty lists are
correct when no qualifying feature exists. Never invent an item to fill a category.
